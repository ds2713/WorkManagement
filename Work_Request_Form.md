# Introduction

The purpose of this work request form is to explicitly state exactly what work is required and in what format, so that all requirements and outcomes are agreed on paper before any work is started. Upon completion of the work, the deliverables can be compared directly against this form to ensure all requirements have been met. The form can then be closed and archived.

To start this work package, we will arrange a short (15 minute) meeting to discuss this request. Before this initial meeting, please complete all the <mark>YELLOW HIGHLIGHTED</mark> sections below. Do not complete the <mark style="background-color:lightgreen;">GREEN HIGHLIGHTED</mark> sections; these will be completed together during the initial meeting.

If you are unsure of anything you need to fill in, give it your best attempt and then we will discuss it together during the meeting so we both have a good understanding. If I am unsure of anything you have filled in, we will also discuss it during the meeting.

If requirements need to change during the course of the work package, this can be done by arranging a meeting with me during which time we will discuss the progress made so far, what needs to change, re-estimate the amount of work required, and agree on new delivery dates.

If other work becomes a priority and the deadline on your work request cannot be met, I will do my best to inform you at the earliest available opportunity. We will then agree new deadlines. However, sometimes this might not be possible if I am requested to help urgently on important projects (e.g. flight delivery).

**Note:** this form is a work in progress so there might be changes to the process once I field test this template.

# Process

1. Copy this template into the David Salmon Work Requests folder with a sensible name.

2. Fill out the <mark>**ONLY AND ALL** the YELLOW HIGHLIGHTED</mark> sections.

3. Arrange a short meeting with me during which time we will discuss the work package and complete the <mark style="background-color:lightgreen;">GREEN HIGHLIGHTED</mark> sections together.

4. I will then go and do the work, and come back to you when I have updates or during the milestone meetings.

5. At the end of the project or when I have completed the deliverables, we will arrange another meeting to test the deliverables against the acceptance criteria, fill out the close-out section and sign off the deliverables to you.
